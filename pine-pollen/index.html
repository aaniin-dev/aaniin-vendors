brand story · wildcrafted in canada

straight from the forest. certified by science.

Every spring, Canadian Pine Pollen's team heads into the pristine wilderness of interior British Columbia to hand-harvest wild pollen from Lodgepole and Ponderosa pines. No cracking, no heat treatment — just fresh pollen, air-dried at low temperature and preserved in its whole-cell form for maximum potency.

Certified organic, GMP certified, FDA-audited, and licensed by Health Canada. They plant a tree for every order. Over 20,000 customers later, the forest is still the source — and quality is still the mission. That's exactly the kind of brand we want on aaniin.

why people keep talking about it

01 / phytoandrogens

Hormonal support, rooted in tradition.

Pine pollen is rich in phytoandrogens — plant-based compounds with researched effects on hormonal health, energy, and anabolic function. Used in Traditional Chinese Medicine for centuries to nourish Qi and support vitality.

02 / purity

Wild-harvested. Never compromised.

Whole-cell pollen, never cracked — because cracking exposes nutrients to oxidation. Third-party tested for heavy metals, pesticides, and mould. Fresh from the mountain to the bottle, with nothing removed.

03 / formats

Tincture or powder — your ritual, your call.

The Health Canada-licensed tincture is fast and convenient. The powder adds clean nutrition to smoothies, tea, or coffee. Both are certified organic, both are wildcrafted. 30-day satisfaction guarantee.
