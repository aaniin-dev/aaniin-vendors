why parents keep talking about it

floor-level by design. built to last.

01 / montessori

A floor that invites exploration.

Montessori beds remove the fall risk entirely. When children are at floor level, they get in and out independently — building confidence before they're even out of their pyjamas.

02 / growth

One bed. Five stages.

The MIMI Nest doesn't get replaced — it converts. From crib to toddler bed to floor bed, five configurations in a single piece. Better for your wallet, better for the planet.

Built from materials your home already trusts.

Solid pine, non-toxic finishes, FSC-certified wood. No off-gassing. No shortcuts. The kind of materials that hold up to a decade of bedtime stories and morning jumps.
